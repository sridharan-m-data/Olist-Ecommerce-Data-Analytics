# 🛒 E-Commerce Customer & Sales Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist** to understand customer behavior, sales performance, product trends, delivery performance, regional performance, and customer value.

The project combines **Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI** to perform end-to-end data analytics and create interactive business dashboards.

The analysis also includes **AI & Machine Learning-based insights** such as revenue forecasting, order forecasting, customer segmentation, churn-risk identification, and Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) analysis.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
* Identify top-performing product categories
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Analyze customer segments and customer value
* Identify high-risk and at-risk customers
* Analyze order delivery and logistics performance
* Identify high-performing regions
* Forecast future orders and revenue
* Generate actionable business insights for decision-making

---

## 📊 Dataset

**Dataset:** Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist

**Source:** Kaggle

The dataset contains approximately **100K e-commerce orders** from the Brazilian marketplace Olist.

### Dataset Period

**September 2016 – October 2018**

### Main Tables

The project uses multiple CSV files including:

* Customers
* Orders
* Order Items
* Order Payments
* Order Reviews
* Products
* Sellers
* Geolocation
* Product Category Translation

These datasets were integrated to perform customer, sales, product, logistics, and regional analysis.

# 👥 Customer Segmentation

RFM analysis was performed using:

* **Recency** – How recently the customer purchased
* **Frequency** – How frequently the customer purchased
* **Monetary** – How much the customer spent

Customer behavior was further categorized into business-oriented segments.

### Customer Segments

| Segment          | Customers |
| ---------------- | --------: |
| At Risk Customer |    68,114 |
| Loyal Customer   |    17,014 |
| Premium Customer |     9,367 |
| New Customer     |     1,600 |

These segments can help businesses design targeted retention and marketing strategies.
